What your site is costing you — Smith Team Las Vegas —

I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way a Summerlin or Henderson buyer shopping for a luxury agent would: find you, trust you, call. The foundation is strong: all 18 pages I opened have their own title, canonical address and real estate agent markup with your phone number, 141 of the 143 photos carry a description, and the search score was 100. The leaks are a homepage that takes 17 seconds to finish loading on a simulated phone, one description repeated on 15 of 18 pages, a YouTube link that goes nowhere, and an award line that reads two different ways.

Checked October 17, 2026

What I found

01

Your homepage takes 17 seconds to finish loading on a phone.

In two simulated slow-connection load tests on a phone-size screen, the homepage scored 32 and 33 for speed. The main content finished showing at 17.3 seconds both times, and the page was blocked from responding for about 1.1 seconds. It transferred 9,254 and 9,628 KiB in 89 and 90 requests, and one file did most of it: the background film (6,624 KiB) downloads as the page loads. The Buyers page has no film and still scored 33, with its main content at 16.1 seconds. Two runs on the homepage and one on the Buyers page; field speed on real phones is unmeasured.

02

One description is repeated on 15 of your 18 pages.

Of the 18 pages I opened, 15 use the identical search description: “Our sensational team is a highly skilled and experienced Las Vegas real estate group, serving luxury home buyers and sellers in Las Vegas.” Only the homepage, Meet the Team and Neighborhoods have their own. Every page has its own title, so a search result can tell them apart by title, but the line under it reads the same for Buy, Sell, Contact, Listings and the rest.

03

Your YouTube link goes to a page that isn't there.

The YouTube icon on your homepage and on Robin I. Smith's profile page points to youtube.com/@smithteamlasvegas-kwlasveg749/videos. When I opened it on October 17, 2026 it returned “not found” (404). The same address is listed as an official profile in your site's markup. Your channel does exist: I found Smith Team Las Vegas | KW LAS VEGAS at youtube.com/channel/UCTh-NA_USujcQW_w4VfgUdQ, so a visitor who clicks the icon lands on an error page instead of your videos.

04

Your “#1 agent” line reads two different ways.

Your homepage says “#1 Keller Williams Realty Las Vegas Agent (2015–2021, 2023), our track record speaks for itself.” Robin I. Smith's profile page says “#1 Keller Williams Realty Las Vegas Agent from 2015–2021, and again in 2023 and 2024.” One of them is out of date, and a buyer or a search tool reading both has to guess which.

05

A few small things trip up phones and screen readers.

Accessibility scored 89 on the homepage and 90 on the Buyers page. The test flagged low-contrast text, buttons and links too close together for a thumb, a skip link that can't be reached with a keyboard, and some elements set to a tab order above zero. The Contact page has no main heading, and the Listings and New Home Construction pages each have two. On the good side, only 2 of the 143 images I counted have no description.

What's working

All 18 pages I opened have their own title, a canonical address and a social-share preview, and 141 of 143 images carry a description. Every page carries real estate agent markup with your name, phone number and address, the server answered in about 130 milliseconds, and the search score was 100 on both pages I tested. Your robots.txt blocks only WordPress's own folders, not search or AI crawlers, and your sitemap index covers pages, agents and communities.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, the real speed on a customer's phone, whether your home search and forms deliver leads (I didn't search or submit anything), and your Google, Zillow and Keller Williams profiles as those sites show them. I didn't call or text anyone.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt disallows only WordPress's admin, plugin, includes and theme folders, with a 5-second crawl delay. It doesn't name any AI or search crawler.

  • Found
    Business markup

    All 18 pages carry RealEstateAgent markup with your name, phone number 702.460.5080 and address, plus links to your social profiles.

  • Found
    Page text is readable without scripts

    The homepage HTML carries about 1,108 words and one clear heading, so a crawler that doesn't run JavaScript can still read it.

  • Gap
    No llms.txt file

    /llms.txt returns “not found”. llms.txt is a newer, optional file that some AI tools read, so this is a small gap.

  • Note
    robots.txt doesn't point to your sitemap

    Your sitemap index exists and lists pages, posts, agents and communities, but robots.txt has no sitemap line.

  • Note
    Two phone numbers in your markup

    The RealEstateAgent markup carries 702.460.5080, while the Organization markup carries +1-725-400-3689, which also appears in the page text next to “NV LIC# 0021625”.

  • Note
    Social profile list includes the dead YouTube address

    See finding 3: the YouTube address in your markup returns “not found”.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 17, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.
